Autonomous Marine Robotics & Systems

Take intelligent exploration into the water.

RESEARCH IN ACTION

Underwater mapping & robotic perception

Marine platforms bring sensing into the water. Underwater mapping, sensor fusion and reconstruction connect robot observations with a spatial understanding of the environment.

Our marine robotics research focuses on remotely operated vehicles (ROVs), autonomous underwater vehicles (AUVs), and unmanned surface vehicles (USVs), integrating underwater perception, multi-sensor fusion, simultaneous localization and mapping (SLAM), and embodied intelligence to support navigation, exploration, underwater manipulation, and interaction with marine environments. Rob Scharff develops soft, bio-inspired robotic fish and octopus-inspired manipulators for low-disturbance seafloor surveying and gentle underwater interaction. Wenqi Hu develops miniature and soft underwater robots, including jellyfish-inspired systems for efficient propulsion, delicate object manipulation, and seafloor litter collection. Ling Shi advances autonomous marine monitoring through networked sensing, adaptive planning, and coordinated control of heterogeneous robotic teams across aerial, surface, and underwater environments. Yajing Shen investigates robotic sensing, actuation, and manipulation, with aquatic robotics contributions including light-driven robots for targeted cargo delivery and underwater vehicle designs for stable, agile maneuvering.
