THE HKUST LIVING LABORATORY

Our campus.
A world of discovery.

The ocean is on our doorstep. Discover the marine life beneath the HKUST pier, where our coastal campus offers a place to observe, experiment and learn. Through the Sustainable Smart Campus Pier project, we are developing a Living Marine Lab that connects marine AI and environmental sensing with coral gardening, education and community participation.
