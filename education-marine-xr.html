A dive you can share

MarineVR opens a guided virtual dive for teaching, demonstration and conversation. MarineAR brings that marine scene into the room. Both are linked from this page.

Photo · 1 of 5

MarineXR offers a level-based virtual-reality learning experience across intertidal shores, coral reefs, and the deep sea. An accompanying augmented-reality application adds real-time, inquiry-based field support and interactive 3D dissection.
