Facilities

Underwater robots, sensors and test tanks the center uses for field work and lab testing.

AField equipmentBTest sites

Field equipment4 items

  • Underwater drone (ROV)

    FIFISH E-GO

    Portable underwater drone for visual inspection and videography.

    • 100 m depth rating
    • 4K wide-angle camera with built-in lighting
    • Hot-swappable batteries
    • Handles 3-knot currents
    Product page ↗

  • Open-source ROV

    BlueROV2 Heavy

    Open-source ROV for custom integrations, currently our test platform for SLAM and VLA research.

    • 300 m depth rating
    • Open frame for easy integration and expansion
    Product page ↗

  • Uncrewed surface vessel (USV)

    BlueBoat

    Open-source surface vessel for hydrographic surveys.

    • Weed-safe propellers
    • Onboard GPS
    • Folds for transport and storage
    • 4G LTE modem for long-range communication
    Product page ↗

  • Doppler velocity log

    Water Linked DVL A50

    Compact Doppler velocity log that measures velocity relative to the seabed.

    • 5 cm – 50 m altitude range
    • Ethernet and serial interfaces
    • Water tracking
    • Web GUI for diagnostics
    Product page ↗

  • Scanning imaging sonar

    Blue Robotics Ping360

    Mechanical scanning sonar for underwater imaging.

    • 50 m range
    • Adjustable scan range
    • Open-source software interface
    Product page ↗

  • Underwater positioning

    Water Linked Underwater GPS

    References surface GPS to precisely triangulate an underwater transceiver’s location.

    • 100 m operating range
    Product page ↗

  • Portable power station

    DJI Power 1000

    Portable power station for outdoor field work.

    • 1 kWh capacity
    • 2× Type-G sockets, 2× USB-A, 2× USB-C PD
    • SDC and SDC Lite ports
    Product page ↗

Test sites4 items

  • Steel-framed pool

    Test pool

    Steel-framed pool for in-water testing.

    • 4.5 × 2.2 × 1.2 m (L × W × D)
    • On-site power and lighting via extension reel

  • Seawater tank

    Large seawater tank

    Seawater tank for testing and observation.

    • 2 × 2 × 1.5 m internal (L × W × D)
    • Large glass windows for observation

  • Flow tank

    Flume tank

    Generates consistent water flow for hydrodynamic testing.

    • Planar walls for optimal imaging
    • Tool-free assembly
    • Double lid for easy access to the test section
    • Adjustable vanes to modify the flow profile
    • Manual or automated speed control
    Product page ↗

  • Seawater aquarium

    Aquarium

    Flow-through tanks with raw seawater supplied around the clock from nearby Port Shelter, keeping local marine organisms for teaching and public engagement.

    • Raw and filtered seawater supplies on tap
    • Supports UG courses and public tours
