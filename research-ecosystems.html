RESEARCH PROGRAM 03

Marine Ecosystem & Biodiversity Intelligence

Study marine life, from coral reefs to microbes.

Our Ocean Science colleagues study coral reefs, marine biodiversity and microbial communities in Hong Kong and beyond. This program connects their research with marine AI and observation at CMI.

3 sub-directions

01Coral Reef EcosystemsOcean dynamics and AI-assisted reef monitoring. 02eDNA & Biodiversity MonitoringTracking marine life from DNA traces. 03Marine Microbial EcologyMicrobes, viruses and plankton in coastal seas.

SUB-DIRECTION 01

Coral Reef Ecosystems

How ocean physics and biogeochemistry shape coral reefs, and how AI can help monitor them.

Research focus

  • Internal waves & reef refuges

    Cooler water that may buffer reefs from warming.

  • Feeding & resource use

    What marine species feed on and depend on.

  • AI-assisted reef monitoring

    Analysing coral reef video with AI.

Led by Alex Wyatt

SUB-DIRECTION 02

eDNA & Biodiversity Monitoring

Using DNA traces in water and sediment to track marine life in Hong Kong, where turbid water limits what divers and cameras can see.

Research focus

  • Water eDNA surveys

    Which organisms are present, from water samples.

  • Sedimentary eDNA

    Past communities from DNA in sediments.

  • DNA metabarcoding

    Many species identified from one sample.

Led by Masayuki Ushio

An eDNA framework for monitoring dolphins and porpoises in Hong Kong.
