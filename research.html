CONNECTING ALL FOUR PROGRAMS

A shared
living laboratory.

HKUST’s coastal campus provides a shared setting for research across our four programs. At the campus pier, the Sustainable Smart Campus Pier project is developing a Living Marine Lab with underwater cameras, environmental sensing, AI-assisted monitoring and coral gardening.

By connecting ecological observation with technology development, the pier creates opportunities to study marine life, test new approaches and involve students and the wider community in marine conservation.
