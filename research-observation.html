See the ocean in new ways.

RESEARCH IN ACTION

MarineEVT & EVT-R1

Marine video understanding connects observations with questions about behaviour, species interactions and ecological events. EVT-R1 uses visual tools to reason about these moments.

What can we learn when we bring different views of the ocean together? We combine marine AI, computer vision and multimodal sensing to connect visual, acoustic, physical, chemical and biological observations. Marine foundation models and 3D/4D mapping help turn these measurements into a richer understanding of coastal environments.
