EDUCATION & ENGAGEMENT 02

Coastal Discovery

Discover the diversity of Hong Kong’s coastal habitats. Through shoreline visits and wildlife observation, learners explore how marine organisms live and interact with their surroundings.

  • Boulder shore field trip

    Students walk a rocky shoreline and look closely at the animals and seaweeds that stay behind when the tide goes out.
  • Mangroves and mudflats field trips

    Field trips into mangroves and across the mudflat to see how a soft-shore habitat is put together.
  • Dolphin boat trip

    A boat trip along the coast to watch dolphins, record what the group sees, and talk about how those sightings are used.

EDUCATION & ENGAGEMENT 04

Marine Research & Citizen Science

Experience how marine science works through educational cruises and Hong Kong Reef Check. Develop practical skills in sampling and ecological surveys, and learn how observations support conservation.

  • Educational Research Cruise

    Time on the water for sampling, observation and conversation about how a research cruise is organised.
  • Hong Kong Reef Check

    Raising public awareness about the value of coral reefs, the threats to their health and the solutions to these problems. Obtaining data on coral reef status in order to allow them to be managed on local and regional scales.
