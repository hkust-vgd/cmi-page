Activity 07

Hong Kong Reef Check

Part of the global programme to promote sustainable management of coral reefs.

In practice

The 2026 check

Divers and the surface team work through the reef-check stations and compare what they recorded.

Photo · 1 of 12

Coordinated by the AFCD, this initiative promotes the sustainable management of coral reefs. Student volunteer divers survey coral conditions and target marine species, generating vital data to assess biodiversity and overall ecosystem health.

While you are here

In addition to coral cover, the divers also need to record the predetermined marine fauna (Indicator-species) during Hong Kong Reef Check. This helps to assess the fauna diversity and the general health of a coral reef ecosystem.
