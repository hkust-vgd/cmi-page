RESEARCH IN ACTION

CoralSCOP

Coral segmentation across varied underwater scenes supports scalable reef monitoring and restoration assessment. CoralSCOP provides an existing example of AI serving coral science.

We connect coral restoration, ecological engineering, habitat enhancement and conservation technology. Long-term field monitoring helps evaluate interventions and develop practical approaches to coastal resilience.
