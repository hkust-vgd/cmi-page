HKCoral

Benchmark for Dense Coral Growth Form Segmentation in the Wild

Coral images taken in real Hong Kong conditions: low visibility, motion blur, clutter and colour distortion. Every pixel is labelled by coral growth form, which helps models generalise to reefs they have not seen.

CoralVOS

Dataset and Benchmark for Coral Video Segmentation

The first dataset for dense coral video segmentation. Segmenting every frame, rather than sampling points, gives more reliable coral coverage estimates and better 3D reef reconstruction.

MarineEVT

Advancing Event-Centric Marine Video Understanding via Visual Tool Reasoning

Underwater video events are sparse, brief and unevenly spread. MarineEVT annotates them as question-answer pairs covering semantic, contextual, spatial, temporal and causal reasoning, with the EVT-R1 tool-reasoning baseline.
